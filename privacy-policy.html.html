Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Welcome to ImageLite. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit and use our website and image compression tool.

1. About ImageLite

ImageLite is an online tool designed to help users compress images and convert supported image formats. You can use the available features through your web browser.

2. Images You Process

ImageLite is designed to process images in your browser. The image compression feature does not need to upload your selected image to an ImageLite server to perform the compression. Your original image should remain on your device when using this browser-based feature.

However, other website features, browser extensions, or third-party services may behave differently. Please review the behavior of any additional feature before using it.

3. Personal Information

We do not ask you to create an account to use the basic image compression feature. If you contact us by email, we may receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message. We use that information to respond to your enquiry and address your request.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website hosting provider or third-party services used on this website may use cookies or similar technologies for technical operation, security, analytics, or other purposes. The cookies used, if any, depend on the services enabled on the website.

You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect some website features.

5. Hosting and Server Logs

ImageLite is hosted online. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, access times, and server requests in its logs for security, troubleshooting, and service operation. Such processing is subject to the hosting provider's policies.

6. Third-Party Services and Advertising

ImageLite may use third-party services, such as website analytics or advertising services, in the future. If these services are enabled, they may collect information through cookies or similar technologies in accordance with their own privacy policies.

If advertising services such as Google AdSense are added, advertising providers may use cookies or other technologies to display or measure advertisements, subject to applicable settings, consent requirements, and their policies.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the website and protect information handled through it. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

ImageLite is a general-purpose image tool and is not specifically directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the matter.

9. Your Privacy Choices

You can choose which images to process, avoid including sensitive information in messages, and manage cookies through your browser. Depending on your location, you may also have legal rights regarding your personal information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website, its features, or applicable requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
