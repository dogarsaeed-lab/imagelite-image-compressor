Frequently asked questions

Is this image compressor free?

Yes. This version runs in your browser and does not require an account or paid API.

Are my images uploaded?

The compression feature processes the selected image locally in your browser. Your image is not sent to a compression server by this tool. Your browser and hosting provider may still process standard website requests.

Why did my image not get smaller?

Some images are already optimized. PNG is a lossless format, and changing its quality slider does not reduce its size in the same way as JPG or WebP. Try another output format or smaller dimensions.

Will compression always preserve the exact quality?

No. Lossy JPG and WebP compression can change image details. Review the preview before downloading. PNG output is lossless for the rendered pixels, but conversion may change metadata or animation.

Does it support animated GIFs?

This browser-based version is intended for still images. Animated GIFs and other multi-frame formats may be flattened to a single frame. Use a dedicated animation tool if preserving animation matters.
